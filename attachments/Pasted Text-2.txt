Common Community App: Business Dashboard and Profile Editor Enhancement

Objective

Improve the existing business dashboard and profile editor to provide a comprehensive, intuitive self-service workspace for local businesses, service providers, retailers, restaurants, event organisers and social clubs.

The current interface includes business statistics, business profile, promotions, media, social media, opening hours, address and contact, owners and staff, menu items and a booking toggle.

Retain the existing visual identity, green and neutral colour palette, typography, mobile navigation and working functionality. This is an enhancement to the existing implementation, not a rebuild.

1. Business Dashboard Overview

Create a dashboard overview containing:

* Business name, logo and main image.
* Business category and operating area.
* Publication and approval status.
* Profile completeness indicator.
* Preview public profile action.
* Edit business profile action.
* Shortcuts to promotions, products and services, bookings and insights.
* Outstanding tasks, approval feedback and relevant notifications.
* Business statistics, but only where real data is available.

Potential metrics include profile views, website clicks, contact actions, booking link clicks and offer engagement.

All statistics must be derived from actual application data. Do not invent or hardcode production figures.

2. Business Profile Editor

Organise the editor into four main groups.

A. Business Details

Include business name, logo, cover image, category, subcategory, tagline, description, public address, map location, service area, contact details, website, opening hours, accessibility and relevant facilities.

Support different business models, including businesses operating from a public premises, appointment-only locations and mobile or home-based services.

Allow public and private contact information to be managed separately.

B. Content and Offers

Include:

* Media gallery with upload, preview, reorder and deletion controls.
* Promotions and offers with title, description, images, terms, dates and publication status.
* Products and services with relevant descriptions and prices.
* Menu management for applicable hospitality businesses.
* Category-specific content fields.

Do not force businesses to complete fields that are irrelevant to their business type.

C. Customer Connections

Include:

* Booking links and appointment configuration where supported.
* Public contact methods.
* Website and social media links.
* Customer enquiry handling where supported.
* Relevant calls to action on the public profile.

Only expose booking management features that are implemented and integrated with the existing platform.
